How It Works
04 / 13
03 — Architecture

From pixel to decision
in under 300ms.

01
Ingest

Pull streams from any camera via RTSP/ONVIF. Edge nodes decode and buffer locally.

02
Perceive

Vision models detect people, objects, anomalies, and events per frame.

03
Reason

Rules engine evaluates context: zone, time, severity, repeat offenders.

04
Alert

Right channel, right person, with video evidence and location.

05
Learn

Feedback loops refine models for your site conditions.

Falcon runs on commodity GPU edge boxes or on-prem servers — no proprietary camera hardware required. Models are fine-tuned per site in under 2 weeks.
